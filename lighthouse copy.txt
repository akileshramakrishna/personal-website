      <h1 class="post__title">Seward Lighthouse</h1>
      <div class="post__body">
        <p>As if destiny and fate collided - and here you are. An infinitesimal grain. Turning each day against the horizon; epic lives in proportion to nothing.</p>

        <p>WWe can think, read, watch - but to live by our duty is action. Exercising courage, strength and faith. Against the fears, the weight, the tides.</p>

        <p>TTo begin from within while the world chases without. The eternal spring of joy emanating from our own, boundless realization.</p>
